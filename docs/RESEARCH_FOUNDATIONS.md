# Research Foundations

Core hypothesis: computational emotional states can emerge from continuous cognitive
processing, accumulated experiences, contextual interpretation and temporal
development, rather than being manually assigned to every response.

This is a hypothesis, not an established conclusion. The software is not conscious,
sentient, or capable of subjective feelings. We distinguish:

- Computational emotional representations.
- Emotion-related cognitive processing.
- Emotionally informed behavior.
- Subjective conscious experience (not established).

Inspiration: human emotions develop through experience, interpretation, memory,
expectation, and time. Realization may make significance recognizable; emotion may
have been developing throughout.
